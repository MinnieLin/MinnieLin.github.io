I am a Postdoctoral Scholar in the [Advancing Learning Lab](https://lab.vanderbilt.edu/advancinglearning/) at Vanderbilt University, working with [Dr. Cristina Zepeda](https://www.cristinazepeda.com/). I received my Ph.D. in Educational Psychology and Educational Technology from Michigan State University, where I was advised by [Dr. Rand Spiro](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=yvqvXt0AAAAJ&hl=en). My research examines how **emerging technologies** can be designed to support **metacognition**, **self-regulated learning**, **cognitive flexibility**, and **complex problem solving**.

My research program is organized around three interconnected lines:

- **Generative AI and adaptive learning technologies:** I study how these technologies can support **metacognitive regulation** and **self-regulated learning**. My dissertation focused on designing and evaluating a theory-driven AI learning partner that helps learners plan, monitor, evaluate, and adapt their reasoning during complex problem solving. I am currently extending this work to **technology-supported mathematics learning**, examining how embedded metacognitive supports influence students' learning, motivation, and self-regulation.
- **Game-based learning:** I study how game design, instructional supports, and individual differences in **cognitive flexibility** shape problem solving and transfer.
- **Immersive learning environments:** I investigate technologies such as **virtual reality**, focusing on how features of the technology and learners' experiences influence engagement, social presence, and learning.

Across these research lines, I integrate **psychological and learning theories** with **user-centered design**, **mixed methods**, and **learning analytics**. My broader goal is to develop learning technologies that help learners become more reflective, adaptive, and flexible when navigating complex problems.
